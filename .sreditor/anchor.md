# Anchor: Building Farpost, a continuously updated authoritative record of a building's facts, maintained by contributors (owners, residents, and professionals) so that current, trustworthy information is available whenever it's needed.

## Revision 2026-07-26 (original)

**Goal:** Building Farpost, a continuously updated authoritative record of a building's facts, maintained by contributors (owners, residents, and professionals) so that current, trustworthy information is available whenever it's needed.
**Genuine uncertainty:** The genuine technological uncertainty is how to reconcile conflicting claims about the same building fact when they are logged independently, sometimes offline, by contributors with different levels of verified trust (e.g., a licensed contractor vs. a self-reporting homeowner), into a single authoritative record. There is no established pattern to draw on, and it is not yet known whether the correct resolution mechanism is overwrite, versioning, or a weighted merge of competing claims.
**Success criteria:** Solved means: (1) an explicit, statable conflict-resolution policy exists prior to testing, precise enough that someone unfamiliar with the code could predict the outcome of a new conflict from the rule alone; (2) a seeded, adversarial test suite with known-correct answers (covering cases like simultaneous offline edits from different trust levels, out-of-order sync, and the same fact edited from two devices) converges on the correct resolved value consistently, not merely without errors; (3) every fact retains a visible history—who claimed what, when, and why the current value won—so outcomes can be audited rather than merely hoped for; (4) a live, measured correction rate exists from independent re-verification (e.g., a returning professional or later inspection checking a resolved fact against reality), with a low or declining rate serving as the real signal of success rather than an absence of checks; and (5) people demonstrably rely on the record instead of re-verifying facts from scratch, indicating that trust has genuinely transferred to the system.
