# Farpost Brand Tokens

Source: NFC business card design session (Field.farpost.ca business cards, June 2026), printed and confirmed via Seritag proof x48618.

## Colors

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#16243D` | Header bands, back panel field |
| Signal orange | `#E8743B` | Accent lines, subtitle text |
| Off-white | `#F3F1EC` | Front card body / paper background |
| Slate gray | `#6B7280` | Secondary text (titles, labels) |
| Hairline navy | `#2C3E5C` | Subtle structure lines on navy backgrounds |

## Typography

| Role | Options | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark ("FARPOST") | Archivo Narrow *or* Barlow Condensed | Condensed/technical display face — "surveyor's stencil" register |
| Name / title / body | Inter *or* Source Sans | Clean humanist sans |
| URLs / tap targets | Monospace | Deliberately code-like, not web-like (e.g. `field.farpost.ca`) |

Note: the wordmark and body fonts were left as either/or candidates at design time, not locked to a single choice. If exact matching is needed, compare against the printed card proof.

## Design register

Functional, durable-feeling, slightly technical — closer to a surveyor's or inspector's card than a SaaS startup card. Navy/orange pairing carried over from existing Farpost NFC plates.

## Print reference

- Product: PVC card, full colour print, Crystal Matt finish
- Chip: NTAG213, single encoding (locked) → `https://field.farpost.ca`
- Vendor: Seritag, order ref x48618
